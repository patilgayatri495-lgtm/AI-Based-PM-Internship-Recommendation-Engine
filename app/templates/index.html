About the PM Internship Scheme

The Prime Minister's Internship Scheme (PMIS) is a government initiative aimed at providing internship opportunities to youth across India. The scheme seeks to bridge the gap between education and employment by offering practical work experience in various organizations.

InternDisha is an independent academic project inspired by this scheme. We help students discover relevant opportunities by matching their profiles with available internship positions.

Important Notice:
  • InternDisha is an independent academic project and is not affiliated with the Government of India.
  • Final eligibility, application acceptance, and selection are determined by the official authorities.
  • Please verify all information on the official PM Internship Scheme portal.
  • Scheme conditions may change. Always refer to official sources for the latest information.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is InternDisha?
InternDisha is an independent academic project that helps students discover internship opportunities through personalized recommendations based on their skills, education, and preferences.
How does recommendation scoring work?
Our recommendation engine uses a hybrid approach combining skill matching, education compatibility, text similarity, and preference matching to calculate a match score for each opportunity.
Is InternDisha an official government website?
No, InternDisha is an independent academic project inspired by the PM Internship Scheme. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India.
