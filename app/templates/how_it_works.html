How InternDisha Works

InternDisha uses a systematic approach to help you find the right internship opportunities. Here's how our platform works:

Step 1: Create Your Account

Register on InternDisha with your email and create a secure password. Your account will give you access to all features including profile management, resume upload, and personalized recommendations.

Step 2: Complete Your Profile

Fill in your profile information across multiple sections:

  • Personal Details: Contact information, location, language preference
  • Education: Qualification, institution, specialization, graduation year
  • Skills & Experience: Technical skills, soft skills, projects, certifications
  • Preferences: Preferred industries, domains, roles, and locations

Step 3: Upload Your Resume

Upload your resume in PDF or DOCX format. Our system will automatically extract information such as skills, education, projects, and certifications. You can review and correct the extracted information before saving it to your profile.

Step 4: Generate Recommendations

Our recommendation engine analyzes your profile and matches it with available opportunities using:

  • Skill Matching (35%): Compares your skills against opportunity requirements
  • Education Compatibility (20%): Evaluates qualification alignment
  • Text Similarity (20%): Analyzes resume/profile text with opportunity descriptions
  • Preference Match (15%): Matches your industry and role preferences
  • Location Preference (10%): Considers your preferred work location

Step 5: Review Recommendations

Browse your personalized recommendations with detailed match scores, matching skills, missing skills, and explanations for each recommendation. Understand why an opportunity matches your profile.

Step 6: Explore and Apply

Explore opportunities in detail, save interesting ones for later, compare multiple opportunities, and track your applications. Apply through the official PM Internship Scheme portal.

Step 7: Skill Gap Analysis

Identify skills you're missing based on your desired opportunities. Get personalized learning recommendations with curated resources to improve your profile and increase your match scores.
