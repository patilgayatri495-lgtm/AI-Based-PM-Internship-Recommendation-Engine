About the PM Internship Scheme

Overview

The Prime Minister's Internship Scheme (PMIS) is a flagship initiative by the Government of India designed to provide meaningful internship opportunities to the country's youth. The scheme aims to enhance employability by offering practical work experience in top companies across various sectors.

Key Objectives

  • Provide hands-on work experience to young professionals
  • Bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements
  • Enhance skill development and career readiness
  • Create pathways to employment in reputed organizations
  • Support youth in making informed career choices

Eligibility Criteria

While specific eligibility criteria are determined by the official scheme guidelines, typical requirements may include:

  • Age between 21-24 years (subject to official guidelines)
  • Minimum educational qualification (typically a bachelor's degree)
  • Not currently employed or engaged in full-time education
  • Indian citizenship
  • Meeting specific opportunity requirements set by participating organizations
Important:

Eligibility criteria are subject to change. Please refer to the official PM Internship Scheme portal for the most accurate and up-to-date information.

About InternDisha

InternDisha is an independent academic project developed as a semester project for B.Tech Information Technology. It uses AI and machine learning techniques to provide personalized internship recommendations to students.

Our platform analyzes student profiles, including education, skills, projects, and preferences, to match them with suitable internship opportunities. The recommendation engine uses a hybrid approach combining rule-based eligibility checks, skill matching, and text similarity algorithms.
