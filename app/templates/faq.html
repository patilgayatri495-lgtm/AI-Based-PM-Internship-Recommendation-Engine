Frequently Asked Questions

What is InternDisha?
InternDisha is an independent academic project that helps students discover internship opportunities through personalized recommendations. It uses AI and machine learning to match student profiles with suitable internship opportunities inspired by the Prime Minister's Internship Scheme.
How does recommendation scoring work?
Our recommendation engine uses a hybrid approach with the following weighted components:
  • Skill Matching: 35%
  • Education Compatibility: 20%
  • Text Similarity: 20%
  • Preference Match: 15%
  • Location Preference: 10%
The scores are calculated based on your profile data and opportunity requirements. A higher score indicates a better match.
How do I upload my resume?
After logging in, go to the Resume section in your profile. Click "Upload Resume" and select a PDF or DOCX file. Our system will automatically extract information from your resume, which you can review and correct before saving.
Does a high match score guarantee selection?
No, a high match score does not guarantee selection. The score indicates how well your profile aligns with the opportunity requirements. Final selection is determined by the official authorities and participating organizations through their own evaluation processes.
Is InternDisha an official government website?
No, InternDisha is an independent academic project inspired by the PM Internship Scheme. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India. Please verify all information on the official PM Internship Scheme portal.
How do I apply for an opportunity?
InternDisha helps you discover and track opportunities. To apply, visit the official PM Internship Scheme portal. We provide the link and help you track your application status.
How can I correct extracted resume information?
After your resume is processed, you can review the extracted information in the Resume section. You can edit skills, education, projects, and other details before saving them to your profile. You can also manually enter information if resume extraction fails.
What file formats are supported for resume upload?
We currently support PDF and DOCX formats. The maximum file size is 16MB. Ensure your resume is in a readable format for best extraction results.
Is my data secure on InternDisha?
Yes, we take data security seriously. Your password is hashed using industry-standard algorithms. Resumes are stored securely and are only accessible to you. We implement CSRF protection, input validation, and other security measures to protect your data.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes, you can request account deletion through your account settings. This will permanently delete your profile, resume, and all associated data. Please note that this action cannot be undone.
How often are opportunities updated?
Opportunities are managed by administrators and can be updated regularly. Sample opportunities are provided for demonstration purposes. In production, opportunities would be imported from official sources and updated as new positions become available.
What if I don't find any suitable opportunities?
If no suitable opportunities are found, the system will explain why and suggest profile improvements. You can use the skill-gap analysis to identify missing skills and access learning resources to improve your profile. Updating your skills and preferences may help discover new matches.
Can I compare multiple opportunities?
Yes, you can save opportunities and compare them side by side. The comparison feature shows organization, title, location, requirements, matching skills, stipend, and other relevant information to help you make informed decisions.

Still Have Questions?

If you have questions not covered here, please contact us through the support section or refer to the official PM Internship Scheme portal for scheme-specific queries.
